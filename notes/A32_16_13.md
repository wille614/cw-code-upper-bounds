# A(32,16,13) <= 26

A binary constant-weight code of length 32, weight 13 and minimum distance at least 16 has at most 26 words.

Read the [full proof](A32_16_13.pdf) or its [LaTeX source](A32_16_13.tex).
The previous text is available in the [v2.0.1 release package](https://github.com/wille614/cw-code-upper-bounds/blob/v2.0.1/notes/A32_16_13.md).
