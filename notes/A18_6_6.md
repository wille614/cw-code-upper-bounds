# A(18,6,6) <= 183

A binary constant-weight code of length 18, weight 6 and minimum distance at least 6 has at most 183 words.

Read the [full proof](A18_6_6.pdf) or its [LaTeX source](A18_6_6.tex).
The previous text is available in the [v2.0.1 release package](https://github.com/wille614/cw-code-upper-bounds/blob/v2.0.1/notes/A18_6_6.md).
